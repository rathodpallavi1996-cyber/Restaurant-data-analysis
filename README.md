# 🍽️ Restaurant Data Analysis & Business Insights

## Project Overview

Analyzed a restaurant dataset using Python and Tableau to understand
restaurant distribution, cuisines, pricing, ratings, table booking,
online delivery, and customer engagement.

The project was completed as part of the Data Analyst Masters Capstone.
Python was used for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, while
Tableau was used to create interactive visualizations and dashboards.

## Business Objective

The objective was to analyze restaurant data and identify patterns that
could help a restaurant platform understand its market, improve
recommendations, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Analysis Performed

- Cleaned and prepared 9,551 restaurant records
- Identified and handled missing values
- Checked and removed duplicate records
- Analyzed restaurant distribution across cities
- Identified restaurant brands with the widest city presence
- Analyzed table-booking and online-delivery availability
- Compared customer votes based on online-delivery availability
- Identified the most popular cuisines
- Analyzed restaurant pricing and cost distribution
- Studied ratings across price ranges and service options
- Analyzed relationships between ratings, votes, price range,
  cost, and number of cuisines
- Prepared cleaned data for Tableau visualization

## Key Findings

- New Delhi had the highest number of restaurants in the dataset,
  with 5,473 restaurants.
- Barbeque Nation had the widest presence across cities, appearing
  in 22 cities.
- 25.66% of restaurants offered online delivery.
- North Indian cuisine was the most frequently represented cuisine.
- Restaurants in higher price ranges generally had higher average
  ratings.
- Restaurants offering table booking and online delivery showed
  higher average ratings in this dataset.
